Our story · Since 1895

Time deepens
our roots.

A century-old tea company, shaped by Assam and strengthened by a continuing belief in its future.

An enduring Assam connection

A long history.
A living business.

Incorporated on 7 January 1895, The Grob Tea Company Limited was once part of M/s. Octavius Steel & Co. Ltd. Its legacy is rooted in Assam tea and the development of its gardens.

The Rawalwasia Group acquired the company in 2009. With interests in telecom, hospitality and polypacks, the group brought a new chapter of investment in modern manufacturing, plantation upgrades, field development and practices.
